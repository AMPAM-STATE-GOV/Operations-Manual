# Public Notices Guide

AMPAM public notices are official communications from the nation to the international community. They carry the full force and authority of Moroccan treaty law.

NOTICE TYPES: general, treaty, diplomatic, legislative, judicial

CREATION: Officials create notices through the Public Notices tab. Notices are auto-numbered AMPAM-PN-YYYY-NNN. Each notice has a title, body, type, issuing department, and effective date.

PUBLICATION: When an official clicks Publish, the notice is distributed through four channels simultaneously:
1. GitHub — committed to the AMPAM-STATE-GOV/National-Gazette repository
2. Telegram — broadcast through the ADDIF bot
3. IPFS — content hash generated for permanent decentralized storage
4. Registry — recorded as a controlled record in Supabase

Any party receiving notice through any of these channels is deemed to have constructive notice of the contents.

The National Gazette on GitHub serves as the permanent public record. Every act, decree, notice, and court order is published there for the world to see.
