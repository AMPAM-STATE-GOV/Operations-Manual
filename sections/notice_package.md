# Notice Package and Filing Guide

The **Notice Package** is the standard set of notices generated by the **NoticeDelivery Agent** at major lifecycle events — registration, encounter, status activation, and consular notification. Each notice carries the chain on its face: **Madrid Protocol 1880 → AMPAM → ADDIF Registry.**

## Why Notices Matter

Notice puts a counterparty on the record. Once a counterparty has been served notice of the National's status under treaty, the counterparty cannot later claim ignorance — and any acts inconsistent with the notice are themselves treaty breaches. The Notice Package operationalizes the Law of Sankofa's affirmation of **Right #8** — *Due Process of Law, with Notice and Opportunity to Defend.*

## The Eight Standard Notices

At full registration, the NoticeDelivery Agent generates eight notices, each addressed to a specific counterparty class:

| # | Recipient | Purpose |
|---|-----------|---------|
| 1 | **U.S. Department of State, Office of Foreign Missions** | Notice of the National's reclamation of protégé status under Madrid 1880 Art. 15 ¶1 |
| 2 | **U.S. Department of the Treasury / IRS** | Notice of fiduciary capacity (Form 56 reference); change of status from domiciled subject to foreign trust |
| 3 | **Social Security Administration** | Notice of repudiation of prior SSN-based identification under the involuntary naturalization |
| 4 | **State Secretary of State (Michigan)** | Notice of the National's Provincial standing within AMPAM Dominions |
| 5 | **State Department of Treasury (Michigan)** | Notice of tax position |
| 6 | **County Clerk / Recorder of Deeds** | Notice of land claim restoration under the four-qualifier title standard |
| 7 | **Issuing bank or financial institution** | Notice of W-8BEN status and trust structure |
| 8 | **Consular post of any foreign state with which the National has a residual relationship** | Notice of restored Moroccan standing |

The exact addressee list adapts to the National's specific situation (e.g., a different state if the National's prior naturalization was outside Michigan).

## Structure of a Notice

Each notice is a controlled record with: header (issuing authority, addressee, date, controlled record ID), treaty citation block (Treaty 1786/1836, Madrid 1880 Art. 15 ¶1, Algeciras 1906, VCCR 1963 Art. 36 where applicable), substantive body, declaration of the National's status (*jus sanguinis* heir, Star tier, ADDIF identifier), refusal of denationalizing labels, 21-day demand for acknowledgment, signature block (National's autograph, AMPAM seal, treaty chain), and verification URL + QR code pointing to the Cert Server entry.

## Filing Sequence

1. **Trigger event** — registration completes, encounter is filed, status is activated, or detention is notified
2. **NoticeDelivery agent** picks up the task from `task_queue` with `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`
3. **Generation** — each notice is composed from the relevant template (`instrument_templates` table) and the National's NMF record
4. **Approval** — the Hajib (or delegated Rasm) signs the notice; signature is recorded as a controlled action
5. **Delivery** — sent via tracked mail (USPS Certified, FedEx, or international diplomatic post); tracking number recorded in `nmf_correspondence`
6. **Response capture** — any response is logged against the notice's controlled record

## Tracking and Escalation

Statuses: `delivered` (tracking confirms receipt — 21-day clock starts); `acknowledged` (counterparty responds within window — closed); `unanswered` (21 days elapse — Compliance Agent flags, non-response is itself recorded); `objected` (Auditor reviews, matter may be referred to Dar-ul'Adl); `breached` (counterparty acts inconsistently — treaty-breach record, diplomatic correspondence by Hajib).

## Compliance and Audit

The Auditor Agent verifies chain, citations, signature, and hash on every notice. The Compliance Agent monitors response windows.
