# Sovereign Immunity and National Defense Protocol

Sovereign immunity is the principle that a sovereign state — and its officers acting in their official capacity — cannot be subjected to the courts of another state without consent. AMPAM, as a Provincial State Government under the pre-existing sovereignty of the Moroccan Empire, holds this immunity. Every record of an immunity assertion carries the chain **Madrid Protocol 1880 → AMPAM → ADDIF Registry.**

## Source of Immunity

AMPAM's immunity flows from the treaty stack documented in the Law of Sankofa:

- **Treaty of Peace and Friendship 1786/1836** — perpetual; the United States and its agents are bound to recognize Moroccan instruments *"without impairment, derogation, or interference"*
- **Madrid Convention 1880** — Article 15 ¶1 protections for Moroccan nationals abroad
- **General Act of Algeciras 1906** — codified Sovereignty, Territorial Integrity, and Economic Liberty
- **Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations 1961** and **Vienna Convention on Consular Relations 1963** — diplomatic and consular inviolability
- **UN Charter 1945** — sovereign equality of states

## Tiers of Immunity

| Tier | Holder | Scope |
|------|--------|-------|
| **State** | AMPAM as Provincial State Government | Full immunity from foreign jurisdiction in matters of state |
| **Diplomatic** | Hajib (Minister of Foreign Affairs), Mufti (Consul General), accredited Ambassadors | Personal inviolability under Vienna 1961/1963 |
| **Consular Court** | Qazi (Justices) of the Dar-ul'Adl | Judicial immunity in the exercise of consular court jurisdiction |
| **National** | Registered AMPAM Nationals | Protégé status under Madrid 1880 Art. 15 ¶1 — protection against denationalizing process |

National-tier immunity is qualified, not absolute: it protects the National's *standing* and *forum* (the right to be heard before the AMPAM Consular Court), not the underlying substance of every claim a foreign jurisdiction might assert.

## Constitutional Defense Authority

The Law of Sankofa places National Defense under the **Sutrah (Secretary for Defense)** with appointed **Shariff (Enforcer)**, within the Wazir Al'Rais's Executive Branch. The Constitution affirms each National's:

- **Right #9** — *"Bear Arms and participate in an official militia for the Defense of the Nation irrespective of any prior legal status"*
- **Right #7** — *"Self Defense when threatened with harm, loss or deceit"*
- **Right #21** — *"Security from abuse, persecution, tyranny, and war"*

AMPAM commits to *"live in a demilitarized region, where Love, Truth, Peace, Freedom and Justice are the Foundations for the Social Order"* (Right #32) — the National Defense Protocol is therefore defensive in posture, not expeditionary.

## Asserting Immunity in Practice

A National asserts immunity through a structured filing:

1. **Special Appearance** — never general appearance, which would waive immunity
2. **Citation** — Treaty 1786/1836, Madrid 1880 Art. 15 ¶1, VCCR 1963 Art. 36
3. **Reservation of rights** — *without prejudice* under UCC 1-308
4. **Demand for the proper forum** — referral to the AMPAM Consular Court (Dar-ul'Adl)
5. **Notice copy** — to the Hajib and the Mufti via the NoticeDelivery agent

## Compliance and Record

Every immunity assertion is recorded as a controlled record in the ADDIF Registry: counterparty, citation, response, hash of the artifacts. The **Compliance Agent** verifies the assertion carries the full treaty stack, and the **Auditor Agent** cross-references the filing against the National's status to ensure the assertion is consistent with the National's then-current Star tier.
