# Dime Messaging

DIME (Digital Instrument Messaging Engine) is the official communication system for AMPAM. Every DIME message is a controlled record — hashed with SHA-256, timestamped, and signed by the sender.

To send a DIME: click the Messages tab, click Drop a DIME, select recipient from the officials dropdown, enter subject and body, attach documents if needed, and send. The message is hashed and stored as a verifiable instrument.

Recipients receive DIMEs in their inbox. They can read, reply (creating a thread), sign (adding their cryptographic signature), or forward. Every action is logged.

DIME replaces email for official communications. Unlike email, DIMEs cannot be spoofed, altered, or denied. The hash proves the message was sent at that time with that content by that official. Thread chains become undeniable evidence.

Send as Email option creates a mailto: link for external correspondence. The DIME record still exists internally as proof of what was communicated.

Drop a DIME — every message is an instrument.
