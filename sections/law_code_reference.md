# Law Code Reference

The AMPAM Law Code is organized into 10 titles covering all aspects of national governance.

Title 1: Identity and Nationality — registration, ADDIF system, pledge of allegiance
Title 2: Estates and Trusts — estate recognition, UCC position, wealth ledger categories
Title 3: Treasury and Revenue — revenue distribution (10/10/40/40), fiscal policy
Title 4: Currency and Banking — ALDC standard, Credit Union operations, Clearinghouse
Title 5: Court Procedure — Consular Court jurisdiction, e-filing, case management
Title 6: Postal Authority — private postal jurisdiction, UPU compliance, registered mail
Title 7: Departments and Offices — constitutional offices established by Law of Sankofa
Title 8: Records and Archives — AMPAM Records pipeline, archival numbering system
Title 9: Digital Infrastructure — PDH system, DID standard, sovereign DNS
Title 10: Treaty and International — treaty chain, jus cogens, international standing

Each title contains chapters and sections. New sections are added through the legislative process — bills are introduced, voted on, enacted, and codified into the appropriate title.

The law code is stored in the ampam_law_code table and is queryable by agents, officials, and the AI assistant.
