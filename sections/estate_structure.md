# Estate Structure and Protection

The estate framework of AMPAM rests on the chain **Madrid Protocol 1880 → AMPAM → ADDIF Registry** and on the express declaration of the Treaty for the Moroccan State Union that the signatory Nationals shall *"reclaim their allodial rights and ancestral inheritance"* and *"restore reversionary estate interests to the Moroccan people — jus sanguinis heirs of the land."*

## Foundational Terminology

The Law of Sankofa (AMPAM Constitution) instructs precise terminology: **"Land, not territory. Earth, not plots. Dominions, not Real Estate. Parcels, not Property."** A National's estate is not "real property" under foreign jurisdiction — it is **Land held Allodial**, recorded as an ancestral hereditament.

## Title Standard

Per the National Trust article, *"all Allodial Claims to Lands within our Dominions shall be held in Allodial, Aboriginal, Paramount and Clear Perfect Title."* Each qualifier is load-bearing: **Allodial** (absolute, no feudal overlord), **Aboriginal** (pre-existing any foreign jurisdiction), **Paramount** (superior to all competing claims), **Clear Perfect Title** (free of encumbrance).

## The National Trust

The Constitution establishes the National Trust as *"an International, Divinely Established, Sovereign, Allodial, Express Trust."* Its purpose is management, oversight, reporting, and accounting for Treasury revenues and distributions.

- **Trustees** — three Matrilineal Nationals, contracted by the Dalil Aziz, reporting directly to the Dalil Aziz.
- **Treasurer / General Manager** — limited delegated authority over the Treasury, reporting to the Trustees.
- **Lien position** — the National Trust is the **1st Lien Holder** on all Lands claimed Allodial by the government and by the Moroccan Nationals.
- **Conveyance** — Trustees liaison with the **Hajib (Minister of Foreign Affairs)** to facilitate Conveyance of Claims and Titles for movable and immovable assets.

## Reversionary Claims and Hereditaments

The Treaty for the Moroccan State Union mandates the Nationals to *"make all reversionary claims regarding Moroccan Estates and the ancestral hereditaments thereby, both corporeal and incorporeal, in all jurisdictions and all engagements of foreign intercourse,"* and to *"seek collections of all taxes, reversionary interests, and forms of reparations established in all Moroccan Treaty agreements, such as the Treaty of Peace and Friendship 1786 and the General Act of Algeciras of 1906."*

## Treasury and Distribution

Treasury revenue is distributed each accounting period as follows:

- **10%** — Moroccan Empire development funds (erudition, government, housing, family support)
- **10%** — Treasury reserves
- **40%** — Governmental Management
- **40%** — Distribution to registered Moorish Nationals (Patrilineal at half the Matrilineal share)

Accounting is by simple debit/credit, not double-entry. *"Money" is gold and silver coinage*; AMPAM reserves the Right to Coin/Mint Money for the provincial government.

## National's Estate Position

For an individual National, estate structure operates as:

1. **Foundational standing** — registration in the ADDIF Registry locks in *jus sanguinis* status as a beneficiary of Moroccan ancestral hereditaments.
2. **Allodial claim** — Land within the AMPAM Dominions (Lat 30.5°–34.9° N, Long 80.8°–85.6° W) may be claimed and recorded under the four-qualifier title standard above.
3. **Land Bank** — the AMPAM Land Bank holds and administers claims that have been recorded but not yet conveyed to individual Nationals.
4. **Foreign Grantor Trust** — for assets currently sited in foreign jurisdictions, a Foreign Grantor Trust framework is the lawful conduit for repatriating reversionary interests without surrendering the Allodial standard.

Every estate instrument — title, claim, conveyance, lien, distribution — is recorded into the ADDIF Registry and carries the chain: **Madrid Protocol 1880 → AMPAM → ADDIF Registry.**
