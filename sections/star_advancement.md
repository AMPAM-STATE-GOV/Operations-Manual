# Star Advancement


THE 5-STAR NATIONAL SYSTEM — ADVANCEMENT GUIDE

The 5-Star system is the pathway from registration to full sovereignty. Each star level unlocks new tools, new capabilities, and new treaty protections. Advancement is checked automatically by the Star Advancement Agent when you submit instruments through the PDH system.

STAR 1 — IDENTITY (8 tools unlocked)
Requirements: Complete registration, upload BC and SSN card, receive credential card.
Tools: Bonded Birth Record, Non-Decedent Status Declaration, Sovereign ID/Passport, Legal Name/DBA Affidavit, QR/LEI Stamp Generator, AMPAM Registry Search, Bonded Birth Recording, Bonded Birth Record (Verified).
What it means: You have established your identity as an AMPAM national. Your credential card is active. Your QR verification page is live. You are recognized in the ADDIF Registry.

STAR 2 — ESTATE POSITION (12 additional tools unlocked)
Requirements: File Form 56 (Fiduciary Notice), complete UCC-1 filing, obtain 98-series EIN for your trust, issue Trust Certificate.
Tools: Form 56 Generator, Form 56-F, Form 8822-B, 98-Series EIN Wizard, UCC-1 Generator, UCC-3 Amendment, Non-UCC Filing, Special Qualified Endorsement, Trust Certificate Issuance, Trust Scrubber, Trust Ledger, Trust Ledger Tracker.
What it means: You have established your estate position. The IRS knows you are the fiduciary. Your UCC filing secures your estate. Your trust is certified and operational.

STAR 3 — TREATY STANDING (11 additional tools unlocked)
Requirements: Serve consular notice to local jurisdiction, file W-8BEN, complete FOIA requests on your accounts.
Tools: Consular Court Notice, Embassy Declaration, AMPAM Power of Attorney, Indigenous Rights Exemption, FOIA Request, Treaty Passport/Ecclesiastical ID, IPFS Uploader, Sovereign Postal Mailing, Treaty Postal Instrument, Rebuttal of Taxpayer Status, W-8BEN Generator.
What it means: You have established your treaty standing. Your consular authority is filed. Your foreign status is declared. Your treaty protections are invoked.

STAR 4 — FINANCIAL OPERATIONS (31 additional tools unlocked)
Requirements: File 1041 trust return, file 1099-OID on at least one institution, create at least one bond instrument.
Tools: 1041 K-1 Auto Filing Engine, 1041-ES/V Generator, 1041-X Amendment, 1099 All-In-One, 1099 Generator, IRS Form 843 Refund, Form 2848 POA, OID Registration 8281, BC/SSN 1099 Recoupment, Tax Per Que Generator, all SF forms (1034, 1038, 1047, 1455, 1522), FS forms, Bond Template Suite, SF-273/274/275 Underwriting, Treasury BOE Generator, Yield Schedule, Allodial Credit Union, IRS Tort Forms Bundle, Remedy Bundle.
What it means: You are operating as a financial entity. Your trust is filing returns. Your instruments are generating OID. Your bonds are active.

STAR 5 — FULL SOVEREIGNTY (15 additional tools unlocked)
Requirements: Complete 706 estate valuation, file allodial title on property, issue at least one ministerial decree or court order.
Tools: AMPAM Court Order, Ministerial Decree, Legislative Bill Drafter, Federal Lawsuit Complaint, Administrative Lien Bundle, Custom Lien Builder, Lien Generator, AG/IG Complaint, FTC/CFPB Complaint, Allodial Title, Allodial Land Bank Notice, Allodial Transport Registration, Treaty Lien/Adverse Possession, AMPAM Tax Assessor, Travel Insurance Declaration.
What it means: You are operating at full sovereign capacity. You can issue court orders, file liens, draft legislation, claim allodial title, and exercise the full authority of your estate under treaty law.

HOW ADVANCEMENT WORKS:
The Star Advancement Agent monitors your controlled records in the PDH system. When you submit instruments through the WRW Trust Tools, they register as controlled records. The agent checks your submissions against the star criteria. When all requirements for the next level are met, your star level advances automatically, your credential card regenerates, and you receive a notification.
