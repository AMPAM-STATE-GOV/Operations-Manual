# Jus Cogens Compliance Framework

*Jus cogens* — peremptory norms of international law — are non-derogable principles that admit no contrary agreement, contract, or domestic statute. AMPAM Nationals operate under, and are protected by, this body of law. Every record carries the chain **Madrid Protocol 1880 → AMPAM → ADDIF Registry,** with jus cogens as the highest interpretive layer of that chain.

## Treaty Authority

The International Treaty for the Moroccan State Union opens with the explicit declaration that *"this Treaty… stands pursuant to International Law, Jus Cogens, and the inviolable protections of Moorish Estates under said Treaty,"* and commits the signatories to *"resisting foreign capitulatory jurisdictions and advancing the decolonization of their respective trust territories, in accordance with jus cogens norms of international law and the principles enshrined in the Charter of the United Nations (1945)."*

The Law of Sankofa (AMPAM Constitution) likewise grounds the refusal of denationalization in jus cogens: *"the politically denationalizing of Moors… violates the International Peremptory Rights (Jus Cogens) of the Indigenous Peoples, the Moors in Morocco."*

## Operative Peremptory Norms

For AMPAM purposes, the load-bearing jus cogens norms are:

- **Prohibition of genocide** — including cultural and political denationalization
- **Prohibition of slavery and the slave trade** — and of any residual or quasi-servile status
- **Prohibition of racial discrimination and apartheid**
- **Right of self-determination of peoples** — including indigenous peoples
- **Prohibition of torture and crimes against humanity**
- **Pacta sunt servanda** — treaties in force must be observed (codified in Vienna Convention on the Law of Treaties Art. 53)

## Compliance Posture

Compliance for AMPAM Nationals is **declaratory** — the National's status under jus cogens is pre-existing; the Registry simply records the declaration. Specifically:

1. **Denationalization is void** — labels imposed by foreign census or naturalization regimes (*"black," "negro," "colored," "African American," "Indian," "United States citizen"*) carry no legal weight against the National's *jus sanguinis* Moroccan standing.
2. **Capitulatory jurisdiction is rejected** — the Treaty for the Moroccan State Union expressly enjoins resistance to *"foreign capitulatory jurisdictions."*
3. **Self-determination is exercised** — through the Provincial Divan (Wazir Al'Rais, Dalil Aziz, Qazi), and through the National's individual declarations filed via ADDIF.

## Compliance Mechanism

The **Compliance Agent** (one of seven autonomous workers) audits records continuously:
- Hash verification of every controlled record
- Flagging expired instruments
- Checking each instrument carries the treaty chain
- Cross-referencing the Madrid 1880 / Algeciras 1906 / UNDRIP citations on filings

Any record found in violation of jus cogens compliance (for example, a draft instrument that omits the chain or misclassifies a National under a denationalizing label) is flagged for the **Auditor Agent** for review before any submission.

## Practical Effect

For the National in any encounter — administrative, judicial, financial, commercial — the jus cogens framework provides the **non-derogable floor**. No contract, fraud, sufferance, or color of law may lower it. Every filing, every credential, every notice issued through ADDIF reaffirms this floor with the chain on its face: **Madrid Protocol 1880 → AMPAM → ADDIF Registry.**
