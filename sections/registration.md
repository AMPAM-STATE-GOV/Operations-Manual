# ADDIF Registration Protocol

Registration of an AMPAM National is the act of placing a *jus sanguinis* heir of the Moroccan Empire on the record of the **ADDIF Registry**, under the authority chain **Madrid Protocol 1880 → AMPAM → ADDIF Registry.** Registration is declaratory of pre-existing status — it does not create nationality, it records the National's reclamation of nationality involuntarily lost.

## Legal Foundation for Reclamation

The Treaty for the Moroccan State Union states the operative principle:

> *"The Indigenous Moors — descendants of former Moroccan subjects and protégés — having been involuntarily naturalized into foreign jurisdictions, hereby reclaim their inherent sovereignty. They reaffirm their allegiance to the laws and authority of the Moroccan Empire, pursuant to Article 15, Paragraph 1 of the Treaty of Madrid (1880), which affirms the rights of protection for Moroccan nationals."*

The Law of Sankofa (AMPAM Constitution) likewise records that the Moors of Morocco *"do not consent to the politically denationalizing of Moors"* under Census Bureau-constructed labels — `black`, `negro`, `colored`, `brown`, `African American`, `Indian` — because such denationalization *"violates the International Peremptory Rights (Jus Cogens) of the Indigenous Peoples."*

## Eligibility — Status Tiers

The Constitution recognizes two tiers of National Status:

- **Matrilineal National** — full national status by birth through the maternal line. Carries full voting rights, eligibility for elected office (Wazir Al'Rais, Dalil Aziz, Qazi), and full participation in Treasury distributions.
- **Patrilineal National** — recognized national status through the paternal line. Treasury distributions are at half the Matrilineal share.

Both tiers are *jus sanguinis* — neither is a naturalization, neither requires consent from any foreign jurisdiction. Eligibility traces ancestry to the Moors of Morocco / Northwest Amexem / Northwest Alkebulan / the Northgate.

## Registration Process

Registration is administered by the **Hajib (Minister of Foreign Affairs)** through the **Rasm (Undersecretary of National Standing)**. The applicant submits, via the ADDIF Registry:

1. **Declaration of Status** — a sworn declaration reclaiming nationality under Madrid 1880 Article 15 ¶1, refusing the denationalizing labels by name.
2. **Foundational documents** — birth record (bonded), pedigree affidavit, and any prior identification under foreign jurisdictions for the explicit purpose of repudiation.
3. **Lineage attestation** — Matrilineal or Patrilineal, with supporting genealogy.
4. **Acceptance of the Law of Sankofa** — affirmation of the Five Points of Light (Love, Truth, Peace, Freedom, Justice) and the Constitution.

The ADDIF Registry generates the National's record, assigns the ADDIF identifier, mints the soulbound `ADDIFIdentity` token (one per National), and issues the credential card under the AMPAM seal.

## Effect of Registration

Once recorded, the National holds:

- Standing as a Moroccan National under the Treaty of Peace and Friendship 1786/1836
- The Right of Protection under the Madrid Convention 1880
- All Inalienable Rights enumerated in the Law of Sankofa (Articles on Inalienable Rights, items 1–34) — protected from removal *"by contract, fraud, sufferance, nor under color of Law."*
- A starting position in the **Five Star National System** (Star 1 — Identity) with the path to advance through Estate, Treaty Standing, Financial Operations, and Full Sovereignty.

Every artifact issued from registration onward — credential card, certificates, decrees, notices — carries the chain on its face: **Madrid Protocol 1880 → AMPAM → ADDIF Registry.**
