# Legislation Process

AMPAM legislation follows a defined process from introduction to codification in AMPAM Law Code.

BILL CREATION: Any official can draft a bill through the Legislation tab. Bills are auto-numbered AMPAM-ACT-YYYY-NNN. Each bill has a title, summary, full text, and committee assignment.

STATUS FLOW:
1. INTRODUCED — bill is drafted and entered into the legislative index
2. COMMITTEE — assigned to a committee for review and markup
3. VOTE — presented to the Dalil Aziz for vote with yea/nay tally
4. ENACTED — signed by the Wazir Al Rais, effective date set
5. CODIFIED — incorporated into AMPAM Law Code under the appropriate title

VETO OVERRIDE: The Dalil Aziz can override a Wazir veto with 67% or greater vote (per Article 2 of the Constitution).

AMENDMENT: Constitutional amendments require 85% vote by both the Dalil Aziz AND matrilineal nationals (per Article 7).

When a bill is enacted, it is automatically published to the National Gazette on GitHub, broadcast via Telegram, and assigned an IPFS content hash.

All legislation is public record under the chain: Madrid Protocol 1880 → AMPAM → ADDIF Registry.
