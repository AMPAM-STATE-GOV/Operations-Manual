# Born Recording Procedure

The Born Recording is a sovereign birth instrument that records a baby in the AMPAM registry BEFORE the corporate state can create a CQV (Cestui Que Vie) trust.

When a baby is born to AMPAM nationals, the Seyaraha (Speaker of the House) and Dullah (Spiritual Authority) sign the Born Recording. This instrument:

1. RECORDS the child as a living heir of the Moroccan Empire under the Treaty of Peace and Friendship 1786/1836.

2. NATIONALIZES the child as a Protected National of AMPAM with full rights, privileges, and immunities under Madrid Protocol 1880, Article 15, Paragraph 1.

3. BONDS the child from birth with a 00,000,000 USD surety bond through AMPAM Surety Holdings and Bonding Trust Co.

4. REGISTERS the child in the ADDIF Registry with sovereign identity credentials.

5. CLAIMS the beneficial ownership of any trusts, accounts, or securities created by the state in connection with the birth.

This PREEMPTS the Cestui Que Vie Act of 1666, which presumes a person lost at sea after 7 years. By recording the child in the AMPAM registry at birth, the child is never absent, never lost, and never presumed dead. The estate is claimed, bonded, and protected from first breath.

The Born Recording form is available in the Gov Dashboard under the Born Recording tab. Officials fill in the child and parent information, the Seyaraha and Dullah sign, and the recording is submitted to the ADDIF Registry.
