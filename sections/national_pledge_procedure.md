# National Pledge Procedure

Every person who enters AMPAM makes a pledge of allegiance to the Moroccan Empire under Madrid Article 15, Paragraph 1. This is not joining a club — it is re-entry and excapitulation, returning to your nation.

The pledge text: I, the undersigned, do hereby pledge my allegiance to the Moroccan Empire and the Allodium Moroccan Praedium Ante Michigan, in accordance with the Madrid Protocol of 1880, Article 15, Paragraph 1. I declare my re-entry and excapitulation to the Moorish nation, renouncing all foreign designations and claiming my rightful standing as a protected national under the Treaty of Peace and Friendship (1786/1836).

The pledge is a legally binding instrument recorded in the ADDIF Registry as the first controlled record in the nationals National Master File. It cannot be retracted.

Two checkboxes must be confirmed: refusal of all foreign labels and diminishing misnomers, and acceptance of the pledge under the authority of the Moroccan Empire.

The pledge is attached to all onboarding flows — no registration is complete without it.
